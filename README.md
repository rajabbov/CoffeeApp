## 📜 From Bean to Screen: The Concept

Most food apps feel like generic spreadsheets with order buttons. **CoffeeApp** was designed differently — as a digital extension of a cozy third-wave espresso lounge. 

From the aroma-inspired color palette to the soft frosted glass overlays, every visual element mimics steaming cups of coffee on dark mahogany tables, while Google Firebase quietly synchronizes the roast catalog behind the scenes.

---

## 🏷️ The Tasting Board (9 Specialty Collections)

The catalog is categorized into 9 distinct offerings, querying live inventory directly from Firebase:

### ☕ Espresso & Manual Brews
- **Hot Coffee:** Velvety Flat Whites, single-origin Double Espressos, Cortados, and Spanish Lattes.
- **Ice Coffee:** 18-hour cold steeped brews, iced shaken americanos, and vanilla sweet creams.
- **Brewing Bar:** Precision manual extractions including V60 pour-overs, Chemex drip, and Aeropress single origins.

### 🍵 Teas & Chillers
- **Hot Drinks:** Ceremonial Kyoto Matcha lattes, authentic spiced chai, and organic chamomile infusions.
- **Ice Drinks:** Sparkling citrus spritzers, wild berry iced teas, and mint-infused refreshers.
- **Shakes & Frappés:** Thick blended Belgian chocolate shakes, salted caramel frappés, and creamy hazelnut blends.

### 🥐 Bakery & Café Pairings
- **Morning Bakery:** Flaky French butter croissants, artisan sourdough toast, and breakfast sandwiches.
- **Cakes & Sweets:** New York baked cheesecakes, espresso-soaked tiramisu, and warm fudge brownies.
- **Gourmet Lounge:** Truffle-seasoned bites, grilled paninis, and chef-curated café pairings.

## 💎 Sensory Engineering Highlights

Instead of cluttering the interface with rigid boxes, CoffeeApp introduces four sensory design principles:

- **🌫️ Live Frosted Glass (Glassmorphism):**
  Layered panels dynamically blur the background in real-time, recreating the atmospheric look of a warm coffee shop window on a chilly morning.
- **⚡ Reactive Cloud Inventory:**
  Zero manual reloading. Any price update, new seasonal roast, or menu modification in the Firebase Realtime Database reflects instantly on the customer's screen.
- **🎯 Dynamic Cup Customization:**
  Fluid size selectors (Small, Medium, Large) that immediately recalculate volume and price on the fly without hitching or UI delay.
- **🖋️ Artisanal Typography:**
  Handpicked cursive and calligraphic typefaces (`pristina.ttf`) providing an authentic, hand-chalked menu aesthetic.

## ☕ The Barista’s Brewing Guide (Getting Started)

Follow these simple steps to build and experience the app locally:

### 1. Select the Roast *(Clone)*
```bash
git clone https://github.com/ibrohim2005rajabboyev/CoffeeApp.git
```

### 2. Warm up the Machine *(Open in Android Studio)*
Open **Android Studio**, choose **Open**, and navigate to the cloned `CoffeeApp` folder. Gradle will automatically sync dependencies with the pre-configured Firebase service file.

### 3. Pour and Enjoy *(Run)*
Select your physical Android device or emulator (API 24+) and hit **Run** (`Shift + F10`).
